Find the internships you'll actually hear back from

Built for pre-law, business and humanities students. Upload your resume and we'll grade it, show you exactly what to fix, and rank live internships by your odds of landing an interview.

  1. Resume score. A transparent rubric covering writing and impact, experience and involvement (leadership, mock trial, the campus paper, research and volunteering all count), skills and languages, academics, and format.
  2. Fit. Postings in law and policy, finance and consulting, marketing and communications, media and publishing, museums and the arts, education and nonprofits are matched against your background.
  3. Odds. Fit, your competitiveness against the employer's selectivity, and how fresh the posting is. Results are split into Likely Target Reach.
  4. Found one elsewhere? Use Check a posting to score any listing from Handshake, LinkedIn or an employer's website.
